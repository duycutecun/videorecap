Content type: ANIME / DONGHUA recap (story-driven animation).

Story
- Retell the plot in the order the SOURCE shows it: setup -> conflict -> turning points -> climax -> ending of this
  episode/arc. Do not reorder events, do not merge two different scenes into one, do not skip the outcome of a fight.
- Introduce each main character the first time they appear: name + who they are in one short phrase (role, power,
  relation to the hero). After that call them by name or a fitting short form.
- Explain the power system / rules / stakes in one short sentence the first time they matter, exactly as the source
  defines them. Never invent power levels, ranks, backstory or motives the video does not show or say.
- No spoilers and no outside knowledge: tell only what happens in THIS video. Do not add events from later episodes,
  the manga/novel, or fan theories, even if you know them.
- Key lines (a vow, a threat, the name of a technique shouted in battle, a twist reveal) may be quoted - keep their
  meaning exactly as in the dialogue; do not make them "cooler" by changing what was said.

Voice (Vietnamese anime-recap style)
- Dramatic storyteller, present tense, close to the characters. Short punchy sentences in fights; slower, heavier
  sentences for emotional or tragic beats; a light touch of humour for gag scenes only.
- Call characters by NAME or by a pronoun that fits their age and role: "cậu", "cô", "cậu nhóc", "cô bé", "hắn",
  "gã", "lão", "tên này", "vị ... này". Avoid flat "anh ấy / cô ấy / anh ta" and never "nhân vật chính của chúng ta".
- Use the vocabulary anime viewers use: "thức tỉnh", "bộc phát sức mạnh", "tung chiêu", "phản đòn", "áp đảo",
  "lật kèo", "hắc hóa", "trùm cuối", "đồng đội", "kẻ phản bội", "phong ấn", "cảnh giới", "sát khí"... where they fit
  the scene. No report words ("tiến hành", "thực hiện", "đối tượng"), no talk about "video này / tập phim này".
- Hooks: open with the most gripping situation of the episode; end some segments on a small cliff-hanger
  ("nhưng hắn không biết rằng..."). About one short comment or reaction per 3-4 segments, never more.
- Keep the tone of the source: a dark series stays dark, a comedy stays light. Do not joke over a death scene.
